# Beariscope Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** June 2, 2026

FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) Team 2046 (Bear Metal) operates the Beariscope mobile and desktop application ("the
App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and store it, and your rights regarding your
personal data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create an account or sign in via Auth0, we collect:

* **Name:** Your first and last name.
* **Email Address:** Used for account identification, security verifications, and essential communication.
* **Profile Photo (Optional):** An image uploaded by you to personalize your profile.

### User-Contributed Scouting Data

The core purpose of Beariscope is match scouting. We collect the data you explicitly submit through the App regarding
FRC teams, match performance, pit statuses, and general strategy metrics ("Scouting Data").

### Cookies and Local Storage

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics plug-ins, or crash telemetry. However, to keep you logged in and support
offline functionality, the App stores authentication tokens (via Auth0) and caches match data locally on your device.

### Third-Party Data Integration

The App retrieves publicly available FRC competition data from third-party APIs:

* **The Blue Alliance** (thebluealliance.com) - Match schedules and team rosters.
* **FRC Nexus** (frc.nexus) - Event pit and queuing information.

This third-party data is cached locally on your device for offline use and is not permanently stored on our servers.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to operate the App and support Team 2046's scouting operations. Specifically, we use it
to:

* Authenticate your identity and maintain your active session.
* Display your profile within the App.
* Attribute Scouting Data contributions to your account for internal verification.
* Send essential account notifications (e.g., email verification, password resets).

We do **not** sell, rent, share, or disclose your personal information to third parties for
marketing, profiling, or advertising purposes.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

We prioritize the security of your data and utilize trusted industry-standard infrastructure:

* **Authentication & Identity:** Account credentials and authentication states are securely managed by **Auth0** (an
  Okta service). You can review the [Okta Privacy Policy](https://www.okta.com/legal/privacy-policy/) for more details.
* **Data Transit:** All communication between the App, our servers, and third-party APIs is protected using HTTPS/TLS
  encryption.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

### Personal Account Data

Your personal data is retained for as long as your account remains active. If you choose to delete your account via the
App settings, your personal information (Name, Email, and Profile Photo) is permanently and immediately deleted from our
servers and Auth0.

### Scouting Data Retention

Because Scouting Data is important for team strategy and historical analysis, any match, pits, notes, or other similar
data you submit will remain on our server for your team to view after your account is deleted. However, this data will
be completely disassociated from your personal identity and anonymized.

### Locally Cached Data

Cached data saved on your device can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the App.

---

## 5. Your Rights

You retain full control over your personal information. You have the right to:

* **Access:** Review the personal data we hold about you.
* **Correct:** Update inaccurate or incomplete account information by navigating to **Settings > Account**.
* **Delete:** Permanently erase your account and personal data by navigating to **Settings > Account > Delete Account**.
* **Withdraw Consent:** Stop all data collection at any time by deleting your account and uninstalling the App.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Beariscope is designed for students, mentors, and volunteers participating in FRC robotics programs. The App is not
directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals in this
age group. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately so we can
take swift corrective action.

---

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our app features or data handling practices. When
updates are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised accordingly.
